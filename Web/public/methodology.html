Data Methodology

MM Rates presents aggregated public reference rates. It is not a money changer and does not offer a transaction price.

Sources

  • Myanmar Currency API: a community market-rate feed. MM Rates reads the feed's own timestamp and rejects the entire dataset when it is too old. Its customer-side buy/sell labels are normalized into changer-side buy/bid and sell/ask values.
  • Manually verified market fallback: an administrator may publish current buy and sell references when the automatic community feed is outdated or unavailable. These entries are labeled manual-market.
  • Central Bank of Myanmar: official reference rates. Freshness uses the CBM response timestamp.
  • CoinGecko: BTC, ETH, USDT, and PAXG/USD. Each asset's last_updated_at is used; PAXG is shown only as a world-gold proxy.
  • Binance P2P: a median reference calculated from a minimum set of usable USDT/MMK advertisements. Binance does not provide a publication timestamp for this response, so collection time is used.
  • Yangon gold: manually entered until a reliable automated source is available.

Timestamps

Source time is when the provider says the value was published. Checked time is when the MM Rates server downloaded it. Freshness and readiness use source time whenever available, so downloading an old dataset never makes it look new.

Validation

Old source datasets, values outside pair-specific ranges, malformed values, reversed spreads, unusually wide spreads, unsupported pairs, and future timestamps are rejected. Sudden movements above configured limits must be observed again before publication. Identical snapshots inside a short interval are not stored.

Missing data

Unsupported or temporarily missing currencies are omitted rather than estimated or fabricated. If USD/MMK market data is missing or stale, the service fails readiness and the PWA explains that market data is unavailable while still showing other usable references.

Converter

Foreign currency to MMK uses the available market buy rate. MMK to foreign currency uses the available market sell rate. The midpoint is used only when buy or sell is unavailable.

Limitations

P2P advertisements vary by payment method, limits, liquidity, and availability. Manually verified rates still require human judgment. Local gold and world gold use different units and market conditions. Always confirm a rate directly with the provider before a transaction.
