Privacy Policy

Effective July 11, 2026

MM Rates is designed to work without a user account. The app does not ask for a password and does not sell personal data.

Information stored on your device

Your display name, language, appearance, and automatic-refresh preference are stored locally in your browser. They are not uploaded to the MM Rates server.

Server logs

The hosting provider or reverse proxy may temporarily record standard technical information such as IP address, request time, requested path, browser type, and error details for security and reliability. Keep these logs only as long as operationally necessary.

Notifications

Push notifications are not enabled in version 1.4.1. No push subscription is created by the current app.

External sources and links

Rates are collected by the server from public third-party sources. Opening the developer website, GitHub, or an email link is governed by that service's privacy practices.

Contact

Questions can be sent to alex@minsithu.org.
