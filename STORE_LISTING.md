# Store listing draft

## Name

MM Rates

## Subtitle / short description

Myanmar exchange rates, gold and crypto references.

## Description

MM Rates brings market exchange-rate references, CBM official rates, Yangon gold, crypto prices, conversion tools, charts, bilingual English/Burmese support, light and dark themes, rate freshness labels, manual sync, and clear source information into one clean app.

Rates are aggregated public references and are not transaction quotes. Verify important values with your chosen money changer before making a transaction.
